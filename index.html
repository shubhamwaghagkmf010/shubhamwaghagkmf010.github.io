Understanding the Work-Based Degree Model (YCMOU & MKCL)

Following completion of HSC (12th) in 2022, I enrolled in the specialized B.Sc. in Computer System Administration program established jointly by YCMOU and MKCL. This degree operates on the "Learning Through Working" (Work-Based Learning) pedagogy rather than purely classroom-bound theory:

1. Real-World Workplace Learning
The industry workplace serves as the primary learning laboratory. Learners are placed in an operational IT infrastructure environment for 3 continuous years (Sept 2022 – Nov 2025 at MKCL) performing productive systems administration tasks.
2. Simultaneous Academic Progression
Theory is synthesized before/after working hours and holidays via MKCL’s structured e-learning framework, earning knowledge credits alongside industry work credits validated by mentors.
3. Dual Tangible Outcome
Upon graduation, learners attain both an accredited 3-year UGC-recognized University Degree (B.Sc. by YCMOU) and 3 years of verified enterprise industry experience, eliminating the traditional experience gap.
